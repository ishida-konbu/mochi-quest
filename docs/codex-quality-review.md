# Evidence-based game quality review

This is a reusable engineering review policy. Follow the applicable Wiki acceptance tests, character references, world rules and phase-specific gates as normative; this document does not replace them.

## When and how to review

For phase-wide work, or a cross-cutting milestone adding substantial assets, actions, environments, dialogue and camera behavior, run seven independent specialist reviewers in waves of at most three concurrently open child threads. For smaller changes activate every materially affected lens; mark others not applicable with the reason. If a phase specifies all seven roles (as Phase 5 does), all seven are required at its final gate. No audit merely for audit's sake. Reviewers must be independent of the author of the relevant implementation whenever supported; if separate agents or visual tooling are unavailable, report the gate as incomplete, not passed.

Before review, freeze the candidate revision/diff, run domain tests, build one local playable candidate and establish a shared evidence manifest (revision, affected IDs and paths, test commands/results, reference images, screenshot/video paths, device/viewport, scene/time/quality settings). Give each reviewer only the relevant specifications and evidence. For new actions capture the entire sequence at normal speed and critical intermediate frames; still images alone cannot demonstrate motion or collision. Allow reviewers to operate independent disposable browser contexts or serialize interactive tests. Use the actual local game and measured evidence; code alone cannot establish visual quality. Do not expose unpublished material or private saves externally.

## Seven specialist lenses

| Agent | Scope and evidence |
|---|---|
| worldbuilding_reviewer | Compare changed characters, items, buildings, actions and UI against approved world rules, existing visual references and the actual images of [ColBase A-1530](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/A-1530?locale=ja) where accessible. Assess silhouette, animal anatomy, gesture, brushwork, composition, materials, historical coherence and regional distinctiveness. The game's established stylization and deliberate anachronisms are legitimate; do not enforce photorealism or arbitrary historicism at their expense. Use official museum references if the primary image is unavailable and mark the unverified portion. |
| modeling_reviewer | Inspect every changed shared geometry key at useful close range and representative combinations: front, side, rear and relevant interior views. Check proportions, silhouette, believable thickness, joints, support, rounded mochi anatomy, functional openings, stairs, tools and food assembly. No crude primitive-only near-field substitutes or floating parts. Sensible LOD/instancing is allowed for distant objects; require convincing close views and measured performance. |
| texture_reviewer | Examine every changed shared material key in actual lighting, relevant weather, close/medium distance and quality settings. Check texture direction/scale, UV seams, color, roughness, normals, material response, wood grain, paper, textile, stone, water, food and mochi. Avoid generic plastic appearance, excessive noise, repeated patterns, unrealistic reflections and lighting-dependent artifacts. Respect the established ink-inspired art direction and reasonable GPU budgets. |
| motion_reviewer | Review new/changed actions from approach through positioning, grip, contact, intermediate stages, transfer, completion, cancellation and return to idle/normal control. Inspect real normal-speed clips plus critical frames across applicable body shapes and settings. Verify weight, anticipation, planted feet, hand/tool contact, timing, continuity and prop ownership; no teleporting props, stretched arms, skipped necessary stages or pose discontinuities. A deliberately supported skip/fast-forward control is different from a broken normal sequence. |
| spatial_reviewer | Check character/prop/environment interaction volumes, collision through the complete action (including swept/intermediate poses), navigability, foot support, reachability, doors, ceilings, sloped ground, furniture, equipment, bath edges, clearance, and cancellation. Test representative body modes including size/shape changes; distinguish explicit phasing mechanics and intentional contact from invalid penetration. Prefer repositioning or walking to unreachable targets instead of distorting anatomy or disabling valid collisions. |
| narrative_reviewer | Read changed player-facing Japanese text and follow actual first-time play paths using only information visible at each step. Check natural Japanese, consistent names/voices, clues, objectives, prerequisites, materials, fees, rewards, branching, cancellation and resumption; prevent undisclosed knowledge and secret-region spoilers in player-facing flows. No new canon. Check that prompts and UI actually guide the next action without designer knowledge. |
| camera_reviewer | Play changed actions and inspect full clips in desktop and touch-representative aspect ratios, narrow interiors, obstruction cases and transitions. Check framing of face/hands/tool/target, visibility at critical contacts, occlusion, wall clipping, velocity/zoom changes, manual control and restoration to exploration after completion/cancel. Prefer stable readable camera behavior and intentional cuts; do not remove useful cinematic effects merely because motion exists. |

Additionally the orchestrator owns engineering correctness, persistence/security, performance and regression gates; delegate to a targeted engineering reviewer when a risky change warrants one. These checks complement rather than replace the seven art/player lenses. Secrets in a client-side public game cannot be made genuinely confidential by UI visibility alone; implement the approved in-game access predicates and do not claim source-level secrecy.

## Coverage and findings

- All changed shared geometry/material/action definitions must have representative evidence, plus every required regional/tier representative and risk boundary specified by the owning phase. Exercise new/old saves, cancellations, repeat actions and mobile/desktop interactions as applicable. Use deterministic fixtures for breadth and real gameplay for appearance and feel. Do not treat a generated data row or a mock screenshot as evidence of playable behavior.
- Use the best achievable art and engineering quality within measured constraints: compare against existing high-quality references, not arbitrary polygon counts. Measure FPS/frame times, draw calls, memory and load times where relevant, on documented hardware/settings; compare equivalent before/after scenes. Desktop emulation is not a physical phone test. Report unavailable metrics or hardware honestly.
- Each reviewer independently returns concise coverage, test/evidence paths and findings with `findingId, severity (blocker/major/minor), file:line or asset ID, region/action, reproduce, observed, expected, evidence, proposedFix`. Distinguish a verified defect, an aesthetic recommendation and an unverified criterion. Report pass only for tested criteria. No duplicated reviews merely for reassurance.
- The root consolidates and deduplicates findings, assigns each confirmed defect to a single implementation owner, and fixes all blockers and major defects before declaring the milestone complete. Resolve feasible quality-significant minor defects and record any residual ones. Have the original reviewer or an independent relevant reviewer verify fixes on the updated candidate; rerun affected tests and visual probes. Do not silently lower requirements or classify serious visual failures as optional. Do not repeat a full seven-role review for an unrelated minor correction; revalidate the impacted scope. If further improvement is blocked by a capability, asset, performance or design constraint, state it and the proposed next action.
- Never publish on the authority of an agent review. The owner must test the local build and separately approve release.
